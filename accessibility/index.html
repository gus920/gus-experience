Accessibility

Last reviewed October 10, 2026

This site should work for you whether you use a screen reader, a keyboard, voice control, magnification, or none of those. If it does not, that is worth fixing and I want to hear about it.

Our accessibility goal

Our goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. We are improving the site and have not completed a full conformance evaluation.

Using this site

  • Use the Skip to content link to move past the header.
  • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls. Open the mobile menu with Enter or Space. Press Escape to close it and return to the menu button.
  • Keyboard focus uses a navy outline and white outer ring to help it stay visible on light and dark backgrounds.
  • The interest form has visible labels, required-field instructions, and contrasting field borders.
  • The three book sample pages have complete text versions below their images.
  • Photographs and product covers have image descriptions. Decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.
  • Each of the three YouTube embeds has a descriptive title.
  • The site respects your device’s reduced-motion setting for scrolling and transitions.

What we reviewed

The October 2026 review covered the homepage, Parent Playbook, Sports, Terms, Privacy, and Accessibility pages. We checked navigation, form labels, keyboard focus, contrast, and page layout at a narrow screen width. Our contrast targets are 4.5 to 1 for normal text, including button text, and 3 to 1 for qualifying large text and essential control boundaries.

Work that remains

  • A full screen-reader review with VoiceOver and NVDA, including form confirmation and error announcements.
  • Accessibility checks of downloadable PDFs, including their tags and reading order.
  • A review of video caption accuracy and any visual information that needs an audio description. The videos use YouTube captions. Ask for a written version if you need one.
  • Further checks of zoom, text spacing, and high-contrast display settings.
  • A separate review of Stripe checkout. If checkout blocks you, email us for another way to enroll and pay.

Tell me about a barrier

Describe what you were trying to do and what happened. You do not need the technical term for it. Email gus@thegusexperience.org.

You will get a reply within two business days. If a fix takes longer, I will tell you when to expect it and give you another way to do what you were trying to do.

Coaching itself

Sessions run online and in person. Tell me what you need in order to take part, whether that is captions, a written agenda ahead of time, extra processing time, or a different session length. Ask before you enroll if that helps you decide.
