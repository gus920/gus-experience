Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

The GUS Experience is operated by What-A-Rush Media LLC. This page explains what this website collects, who it goes to, and how to have it removed. If anything here is unclear, email gus@thegusexperience.org and ask.

The short version. We collect a name, an email address, and a grade level, and only when you type them into our interest form. We do not run analytics. We do not set advertising cookies. We do not sell or rent anything to anyone.

What we collect

One form on this site collects information, the interest form. It asks for:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Grade level

That is all. We do not ask for a home address, a phone number, a date of birth, or payment details.

Where that form goes

The form is delivered by a service called FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which passes the message to our email inbox. Your entry travels through FormSubmit's systems on the way to us. We do not store it anywhere else.

What we do not do

  • No Google Analytics or any other visitor tracking
  • No advertising pixels and no retargeting
  • No cookies set by this website itself
  • No selling, renting, or trading of your information
  • No mailing list you did not ask to join

Young people

This coaching is for teenagers, so we expect a parent or guardian to be involved. If you are under 13, please have a parent or guardian fill in the form instead of doing it yourself.

We do not knowingly collect information from a child under 13 without a parent or guardian taking part. If you are a parent and you believe your child sent us something, email gus@thegusexperience.org and we will delete it.

How we use what you send

To reply to you and to talk about coaching. Nothing else. We do not add you to a marketing list because you asked a question.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries while a conversation is active and for our own records afterwards. Ask us to delete yours and we will.

Your choices

Email gus@thegusexperience.org to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Say what you want and we will do it. No form to fill in.

Changes to this page

If what we collect changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top will tell you when.
